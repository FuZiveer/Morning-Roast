About Morning Roast

Morning Roast is a free browser-based utility built for players who care about mouse feel. Whether you are switching from CS2 to Valorant, tuning eDPI for a new main, or warming up with aim drills, everything runs locally in your browser — fast, private, and always available.

Built for consistency Match your muscle memory across dozens of supported titles. Convert sensitivity between games, compare eDPI values, and keep one familiar feel no matter what you queue.
Privacy by design No account required. Conversions and calculations happen on your device. Preferences and aim-trainer scores stay in your browser — we do not collect your sensitivity data.
More than a converter Train reaction time in the built-in aim trainer, convert CS2 and Valorant crosshair codes, browse lineup references, track personal bests on your profile, and tune the entire app to your taste.
Make it yours Pick accent colors, fonts, background patterns or full-screen wallpapers, and a frosted glass theme. Morning Roast adapts to your setup without getting in the way.

How it works
  1. Pick your tool Use the sidebar or the cards above to open the converter, eDPI calculator, aim trainer, or any misc page.
  2. Enter your numbers Select games from the searchable dropdowns, type sensitivity and DPI, or paste a crosshair code — results update as you go.
  3. Copy, share, or train Copy results with one click, share a link with your values pre-filled, or hop into the aim trainer to put your settings to work.
  4. Everything stays local Your preferences and scores are stored in your browser. Clear site data anytime — no cloud account tied to your aim settings.
Keyboard shortcuts

Power users can jump around without touching the mouse. Press 1 for Home, 2 to cycle Tools, 3 for Profile, 4 for Settings, 5 to cycle More pages, and C to copy on supported tabs.

Privacy Policy
Everything you need to know about how our converter protects your information.
1. Data Collection

We believe in privacy by design. Because our sensitivity converter is a utility tool, we prioritize the following:

No Account Needed

You can use every conversion and aim tool without an account or sign-up. We don't collect names, emails, or passwords.

Local Processing

The sensitivity conversion calculations are performed locally within your web browser. We do not transmit your sensitivity settings or game data to our servers.

Stored On Your Device

Your preferences and aim-trainer scores are saved locally in your browser only. They never leave your device, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

2. Usage Data & Analytics

Usage Data & Analytics

To help us improve the performance of our tool, we may collect anonymous, non-identifiable usage data, such as:

  • The type of browser or device you are using.
  • The frequency of visits to our tool.
  • Technical errors that may occur.

This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you individually.

3. Cookies

Cookies

We may use functional cookies to remember your preferred settings (such as your chosen game or sensitivity units) to make your next visit more efficient. These cookies do not track your activity across other websites.

      Advanced Settings

      Training Mode

      Pick the drill that matches what you want to train. Static and Micro build precision, Shrink rewards fast clicks before targets shrink, Track follows a moving target, Flick gives one at a time, Switch jumps between two random spots, and Strafe uses side-to-side motion.

      Track Target HP

      Limited HP lets the target die after 250 damage and respawn. Unlimited keeps one target alive for pure tracking practice.

      Session Timer

      How long each run lasts before your score is recorded. Shorter sessions are great for quick warm-ups; longer ones give a more reliable read on your accuracy and consistency.

      Aspect Ratio

      Pick a canvas shape that matches how you play. Taller ratios like 4:3 and 5:4 feel wider in the trainer; ultrawide 21:9 feels narrower. Fullscreen expands to your whole screen.

      Target Color

      Switch between red, yellow, and purple targets. Use whichever color is easiest to spot while you train.

      Target Size

      Set how big targets appear on screen. Easy targets are larger, hard targets are smaller — size stays consistent across every game.

      Health Bar

      Show a small bar above each target that shrinks as its health drops. Turn it off for a cleaner screen when you do not need HP feedback.

      Red
      Health Text

      Show or hide the numeric health above targets. When the bar is off, the number sits in the bar slot.

      Hitmarker

      Flash an X on the target when you finish it off. Only appears on eliminations — not on partial hits or misses.

      Target Spread

      Control how far from the center targets can spawn. Tighter keeps them near your crosshair; wider pushes them toward the edges of the screen.

      Balanced Recommended
      Sensitivity Finder

      Runs a guided series of 10 sessions at slightly different sensitivities, then compares your scores to suggest the setting where you perform best. Use the reset icon to start the search over.

      Sensitivity Randomizer

      Slightly varies your sensitivity each session so you can't rely on autopilot muscle memory, training your brain to adapt and aim reactively. Best left off when chasing personal-best scores.
